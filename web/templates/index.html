Scoring district maps for fairness.

This page evaluates a U.S. district map against the standard quantitative fairness metrics: compactness, partisan symmetry, competitiveness, county splits, and population deviation. It does not adjudicate intent, race-based claims, or VRA compliance — those require evidence and disaggregation this tool does not have.

For details on every metric, what it captures, and where it fails, see the methodology page. To score a plan yourself, upload a VTD-equivalency CSV.
